I’ve just celebrated one year living in Ireland. I remain thrilled and grateful to be here and am setting down roots.

Since moving, I changed my domain name from <u>novapatch.is</u> to <u>novapatch.ie</u>. I *am* in Ireland after all and *not* in Iceland. I’ve also started maintaining and contributing to this site again.

In June, I presented *[Queer Localization](/en/queer-localization)* at [LocWorld55](https://locworld.com/events/locworld55-dublin-2026/) in Dublin, and in October I’ll present *State of the Regex 2026* at the [Unicode Technology Workshop](https://www.unicode.org/events/utw/2026/) (UTW) in Nancy, France. I’m also drafting a technical proposal to standardize Unicode Grapheme Cluster Properties.

After completing twelve monthly 100 km rides with Randonneurs USA (RUSA) and earning the [P-12](https://rusa.org/pages/award-p12) award in June 2025, I immediately started riding twelve monthly 200 km rides with Audax Ireland to earn the [RRTY](https://www.audaxireland.org/audax/awards-medals/randonneur-round-the-year-rrty/) award, with plans to complete it by riding the [Mick Byrne 200](https://www.audaxireland.org/events-calendar/gazetteer/200km-events/mick-byrne-200/) in the Wicklow Mountains this month. Next I’m planning my first [Super Randonneur](https://www.audax-club-parisien.com/organisation/brm-monde/) series of 200, 300, 400, and 600 km rides in a single year.

I’d never previously considered myself a runner but picked it up over the past year to regularly run the coastal cliff trails and rocky hills of Howth Head. I’ve since raced in the [Gaelforce 10K](https://gaelforceevents.com/en/gaelforce-10k-trail-run-trilogy) trail run trilogy in Howth and Bray, with Wicklow following later this year.
