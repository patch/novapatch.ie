I’ve just celebrated one year living in Ireland. I remain thrilled and grateful to be here and am setting down roots.

Since moving here, I changed my domain name from `novapatch.is` to `novapatch.ie`. I *am* in Ireland after all and *not* in Iceland.

In June, I presented *[Queer Localization](/en/queer-localization)* at [LocWorld55](https://locworld.com/events/locworld55-dublin-2026/) in Dublin, and in October I’ll present *State of the Regex 2026* at the [Unicode Technology Workshop](https://www.unicode.org/events/utw/2026/) (UTW) in Nancy, France. I’m also drafting a technical proposal to standardize Unicode Grapheme Cluster Properties.

After completing twelve monthly 100 km rides with Randonneurs USA (RUSA) and earning the [<abbr title="Populaire">P</abbr>-12](https://rusa.org/pages/award-p12) award in June 2025, I immediately started riding twelve monthly 200 km rides with Audax Ireland in order to earn the [<abbr title="Randonneur Round the Year">RRTY</abbr>](https://www.audaxireland.org/audax/awards-medals/randonneur-round-the-year-rrty/) award, with plans to complete it by riding the [Mick Byrne 200](https://www.audaxireland.org/events-calendar/gazetteer/200km-events/mick-byrne-200/) in the Wicklow Mountains later this month.
